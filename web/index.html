Play Motocross Madness 2

The original game: its own bikes, tracks, menus and sound.

I have the game Pick your installed game folder, your CD, or a CD image or .zip. Nothing is uploaded: the files stay on this device. You can also drop them on this page.
No copy? Try the demo Microsoft's free demo from 2000: the original bikes, sound and menus on two real MM2 tracks. The Free Pack fills in the other events, so everything is open. First time only: the 43 MB demo plus the Free Pack (about 500 MB). After that, Play starts straight away.
Or play the free version

All-new art and sound made for OpenMCM2, free for anyone. No MM2 files needed.

The Free Pack downloads once and stays in this browser. After that, Play only fetches the files that changed in an update. It's big (several hundred MB), so a computer with plenty of memory works best; phones may struggle.

Want more tracks? The demo has two tracks of its own. Players have made thousands more. The starter set is ten of the most raced: a National, five Supercross tracks, three Baja maps and an Enduro map.

    Tracks made by players

    Thousands of tracks made by players are on MDX Racing, and each belongs to the player who made it. Download a track's .zip there, then add it here with the event it's for. It shows up in the game's track list for that event, and stays in this browser.

      Make your own

      The Course Builder lays out a track of your own: draw the line, drop in jumps, shape the land and ride it straight away. Open it from Tools on this page, or in the game from the shovel on the main menu's chain (or Options, Tweaks). Your tracks stay in this browser and join the track lists.
