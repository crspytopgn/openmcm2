OpenMCM2

Motocross Madness 2, running in your browser.

How do you want to play?

Menus

It plays from your own copy of the game: pick your installed game folder (the one with the UI, RES and TERAFORM folders in it). Nothing is uploaded; the files stay on this device. You can also pick your game CD's image (.iso, .bin, .img, .mdf or .nrg), the CD's own folder, or a .zip of the game folder or of a CD image, which is the way on a phone or tablet. Or drop any of them on this page.

No copy of the game? Play Microsoft's free Motocross Madness 2 demo from 2000, with the original graphics on its two tracks. The Free Pack fills in the rest, so every event is open.

The Free Pack downloads once and stays in this browser. After that, Play only fetches the files that changed in an update. It's big (several hundred MB), so a computer with plenty of memory works best; phones may struggle. Your MM2 files stay remembered, so you can switch back any time.

Make your own track and ride it. It works with either graphics, and it's on the game's main menu too (the shovel on the chain).

Tracks made by players

Thousands of tracks made by players are on MDX Racing, and each belongs to the player who made it. Download a track's .zip there, then add it here with the event it's for. It shows up in the game's track list for that event, and stays in this browser.

    Make your own

    The Course Builder lays out a track of your own: draw the line, drop in jumps, shape the land and ride it straight away. Open it with the button near the top of this page, or in the game from the shovel on the main menu's chain (or Options, Tweaks). Your tracks stay in this browser and join the track lists.

    Something not working? and paste it in a new issue on GitHub.
