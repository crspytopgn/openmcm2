Main menu

Pick an event from Single Player to race it on its own, or start a Pro Circuit career. Multiplayer races other people online or on your network. Options, High Scores, Replays and Credits are on the left, and the Course Builder button opens the track editor.

Profiles

Your profile keeps your name, options, keys, careers and records. The first time you play the game asks for a name. Use the profile pull-down to make another profile, switch between them, or delete one. Each person who plays on the same computer can have their own.

Single player

The Event page sets up the race. Choose the track with the arrows beside its name or open the list, then the number of computer riders and laps. Event Type switches between the events, and Race Mode picks the kind of race, such as a single race, practice or a ghost race.

Supercross
Laps of a stadium track, packed with jumps and whoops. Stay inside the course markers.
Nationals
Laps of an outdoor track. Cut the course for too long and you are put back on it.
Baja
Point to point across open country through checkpoints. The arrow and radar show the way to the next one; any line between them is fine.
Enduro
Gates spread over the land, ridden in order. Random gates give you a new route each time, and the seed number lets you share a route with a friend.
Stunt Quarry
Score as many points as you can before the clock runs out. Big air, long jumps and tricks held through the landing score best.

The difficulty buttons set how fast the computer riders are. Vegetation Collision makes trees and bushes solid, and Record race keeps a replay. Press the flag to start.

Bike & Rider

Pick your rider's outfit with the top arrows and your bike with the bottom ones. The engine size sets which class you race in. Type your plate number in the box, and untick Auto Rotate to stop the bike turning.

The wrench (Tune) opens the Garage, where you set the engine's power curve and the suspension (see Garage). Kit & Paint lets you pick your own colours for the high-detail kit, helmet and bike.

Race Info

After a race this page shows where everyone finished, with their times and best laps. View Replay plays the race back if it was recorded.

Pro Circuit

A career across a season of races. You start in the smaller class with a little money, win prize money and stunt bonuses, and pay for repairs and medical bills after crashes. Do well enough and sponsors offer you deals and a ride in a bigger class. Your standings and the next race are on their own pages; your career is saved after each race.

Multiplayer

Host or Join lists the races open now: online races, the always-open free roam rooms, and races on your own network. Pick one to join, or host your own and share its race code. Everyone presses Ready, then the host starts. Press / in a race to chat.

Moto-Tag is a game of tag on bikes, for multiplayer races. Tracks you are missing can be downloaded from the host in the lobby.

Options

The tabs along the top switch pages. Changes are kept when you leave with the back button. Restore puts a profile's options back as they were, Advanced holds the extra graphics settings, and Tweaks holds OpenMCM2's own optional settings.

Game

Units, the race panel, the camera, the menu animations and similar game settings.

Graphics

Pick the display resolution and the overall graphics quality, and turn shadows, particles (dust and dirt) and the sky on or off.

Advanced

Separate sliders for how far you can see and how much detail the ground, objects and plants have, plus the original game's repair settings. The defaults suit almost every computer.

Sound

Turn the menu sounds and the in-game sounds on or off, and set their volumes. Quality chooses the 8-bit or 16-bit engine and effect sounds: 16-bit sounds better and is the default. Waypoint Confirmation plays a chime at each checkpoint or gate.

Controls

Pick the keyboard, a game pad or a joystick, then click an action and press the key or button you want for it. Game pads (Xbox, PlayStation and others) work straight away.

Playing in a browser? Plug in or pair your pad, then press any button on it. Browsers only show a pad to a page after one of its buttons is pressed, so until then the game can't see it. The start page says when it's found, and the pad shows up on this page by name.

Messages

Ready-made chat lines for multiplayer races. Edit them here, then send one in a race by holding Ctrl and pressing its number.

Garage

Tune your bike for each engine class. The sliders at the top shape the engine's power at each engine speed: more at low revs pulls harder out of corners, more at high revs gives a higher top speed. Below them, set the front and rear suspension: damping, and how stiff it is when fully compressed and when stretched out. Use the arrows by the name to pick a saved preset. Softer suspension soaks up bumps; stiffer is steadier on big landings.

High scores

The tabs pick the event, and the list shows each track you have raced, with its best laps and races. Records are kept for each profile on this computer, so a track shows up once you have finished a race on it.

Online shows the lap leaderboards everyone shares: pick a track to see the fastest laps in every class. Laps count from the second lap of a race, ridden with the original handling, and in Nationals and Supercross they must stay on the course.

Replays and ghosts

Recorded races are listed under Replays; pick one to watch it again with any camera. A ghost race puts you on the track against a see-through rider of your best lap. Beat it and the game asks whether to keep your new lap as the ghost.

Where your saves are kept

On a computer, profiles, careers, records, replays and your own tracks are kept in your user folder (~/Library/Application Support/MM2 on a Mac, %APPDATA%\MM2 on Windows, ~/.local/share/mm2 on Linux).

In the browser version they are kept in your browser's own storage for the site, and are there again the next time you open it in the same browser. They are lost if you clear the site's data or play in a private window, and they don't move between browsers or devices.
